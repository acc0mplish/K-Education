# BatiOffice modifications

BatiOffice is a modified distribution of GenOffice. The original GenOffice
copyright, Apache License 2.0 terms, and NOTICE attribution remain in this
distribution.

Material BatiOffice changes include:

- BatiOffice product branding, application identifiers, icons, and packaging
- selectable direct, local, and future Bati Cloud AI provider boundaries
- operating-system protected provider credentials and desktop account sessions
- shared AI conversation, execution evidence, privacy, and certification layers
- HWP/HWPX support based on the separately licensed rhwp snapshot
- Bati Workspace, workflow, telemetry, update, and enterprise integration seams
- reliability, security, upstream synchronization, and release automation

The source repository and Git history identify the exact files and revisions
changed from the tracked GenOffice upstream. No GenOffice or Genspark trademark
rights are granted by the Apache License 2.0.
